## 📋 App Store Requirements

### iOS App Store Connect
- ✅ Privacy policy URL required for submission
- ✅ Must be publicly accessible (not behind login)
- ✅ Should cover all data collection practices
- ✅ Must be in English (or localized versions)

### Google Play Console
- ✅ Privacy policy URL required for apps that collect user data
- ✅ Must be publicly accessible
- ✅ Should cover all data collection practices
- ✅ Must be in English (or localized versions)

## 🔍 What Your Privacy Policy Should Cover

Based on your app's features, ensure your privacy policy includes:

### 1. **Data Collection**
   - ✅ User-generated content (community reflections, chat messages)
   - ✅ Audio/video recordings (for scholarship energy exchange)
   - ✅ Usage analytics (if using Sentry or other analytics)
   - ✅ Payment information (handled by RevenueCat/App Store)

### 2. **Third-Party Services**
   - ✅ **Firebase** (Firestore, Storage) - data hosting
   - ✅ **Google Gemini AI** (Anua) - AI chat interactions
   - ✅ **ElevenLabs** - voice synthesis
   - ✅ **RevenueCat** - payment processing
   - ✅ **Sentry** (if enabled) - error tracking
   - ✅ **App Store/Play Store** - payment processing

### 3. **Data Storage & Security**
   - ✅ Where data is stored (Firebase)
   - ✅ How data is secured
   - ✅ Data retention policies

### 4. **User Rights**
   - ✅ Right to access data
   - ✅ Right to delete data
   - ✅ Right to opt-out (if applicable)
   - ✅ How to contact for privacy concerns

### 5. **Children's Privacy**
   - ✅ COPPA compliance (if app is for children under 13)
   - ✅ Age restrictions (if applicable)

### 6. **International Users**
   - ✅ GDPR compliance (if serving EU users)
   - ✅ CCPA compliance (if serving California users)

## 🎯 Recommended Additions (If Not Already Included)

### 1. **AI/ML Data Usage**
   Since you use Gemini AI (Anua):
   - Clarify that chat interactions may be used to improve responses
   - Explain community cache system (user questions/responses stored)
   - Option to opt-out of data sharing (if you want to offer this)

### 2. **Community Content**
   Since users can post reflections:
   - Explain that community posts are visible to other users
   - Explain moderation policies
   - Explain data retention for community content

### 3. **Video Recording**
   Since scholarship users can record videos:
   - Explain that videos are stored locally and shared directly (not hosted)
   - Explain that videos are not stored on your servers
   - Explain social media sharing implications

### 4. **Audio Data**
   Since you stream audio from Firebase:
   - Explain that audio files are streamed (not downloaded)
   - Explain caching policies
   - Explain any analytics on audio usage

## 📝 Optional: In-App Privacy Policy Link
